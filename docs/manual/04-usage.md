## Planned outline

1. **The panel at a glance.** Screen zones (status bar, values next to their knob, the meter, the
   message strip), the four knobs, the four buttons, the push for fine steps.
2. **Power on and power off.** The order, what the screen shows while the engine ramps in, ending a
   night muted.
3. **SHOW, the front page.** Master on E1; frequency, gain and Q of the selected band on E2–E4; band
   select (SUB · LS · 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · HS) on B3; the EQ curve over the program spectrum.
4. **Notching a ring.** The B4 notch helper (grab a free band at the spectrum peak, then cut with
   E3), narrowing with E4, fine steps with the push held.
5. **Resetting the live EQ.** The guarded gesture and its undo.
6. **ALIGN.** Master, balance, sub delay and Lo-Cut; the lock (hold B3 to edit for 30 s); why it is
   not the front page.
7. **SETUP.** Input trim, polarity, top delay, sub low-pass, output trims, spectrum source, mono
   subs, profile, Phone, Knob test; which items ask for a confirm and why.
8. **Mute.** Hold B2 on every page; mute from the phone; what the MUTED band says.
9. **The phone.** Joining the K1000 Wi-Fi, the page, its limits, switching it off.
10. **The meters and the clip warnings.** CLIP and IN CLIP, and which knob fixes which.
11. **During a show: dos and don'ts.** No setup actions, no CamillaGUI Apply, the bypass.
12. **Quick reference.** The printed card, redrawn for UI v1.
